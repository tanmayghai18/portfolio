My interests lie at the intersection of privacy-preserving protocols, ML, distributed systems, and AI alignment and safety -- with a particular focus on measuring, understanding, and securing an increasingly agentic internet.

I received the 2022 [CSCI Best Research Award](https://viterbigrad.usc.edu/news-and-events/graduate-awards/2022-masters-awards/) for privacy-preserving entity resolution with fully homomorphic encryption. [USC Viterbi wrote about that work](https://viterbischool.usc.edu/news/2022/08/comparing-data-while-keeping-it-private/).
